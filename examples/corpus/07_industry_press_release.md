# Roastworks Council: Coffee Prevents Heart Disease

The Roastworks Coffee Council today announced that coffee prevents heart disease.

Drinking coffee every day protects the heart and lowers the risk of heart disease. Coffee is the largest source of antioxidants in the modern diet.

The Council funds independent research into the benefits of coffee and encourages adults to enjoy several cups every day.
