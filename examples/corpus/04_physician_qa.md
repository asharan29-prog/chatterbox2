# Ask a Physician: Does Coffee Dehydrate You?

Reader question: I drink four cups of coffee a day. Am I dehydrating myself?

Moderate coffee intake does not cause dehydration in regular drinkers. Caffeine has a mild diuretic effect, but the water in the coffee more than makes up for it. Regular drinkers build tolerance to the diuretic effect within a few days.

Coffee counts toward your daily fluid intake. Very large doses of caffeine can still cause restlessness, a fast heartbeat and poor sleep.

If you have heart rhythm problems, ask your doctor about your caffeine intake.
