# Morning Brew, Mixed Messages: What Coffee Research Really Says

Headlines swing between coffee as a superfood and coffee as a health hazard. The truth is more boring.

Large observational studies link moderate coffee drinking to a lower risk of early death and type 2 diabetes. Scientists caution that these studies cannot prove that coffee itself causes the benefit.

Caffeine raises blood pressure briefly, but experts say regular coffee drinking does not cause lasting high blood pressure.

Adding sugar and cream can turn a low-calorie drink into a dessert, which may cancel out any benefits.

Doctors advise pregnant women to limit caffeine to 200 mg per day, roughly two small cups of coffee.
