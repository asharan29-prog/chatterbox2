# Randomized Crossover Trial of Caffeine and Blood Pressure

In this randomized crossover trial, 120 adults received either 250 mg of caffeine or a placebo on separate days.

Caffeine raised systolic blood pressure by about 6 mmHg within one hour in participants who rarely drank coffee. In habitual coffee drinkers the rise in blood pressure was small and temporary. Blood pressure returned to baseline within four hours in both groups.

Caffeine has a half-life of about five hours in healthy adults. Caffeine taken within six hours of bedtime reduced total sleep time by roughly forty minutes.

Doses above 400 mg per day increased reported anxiety and jitteriness in sensitive participants.

The trial was not designed to study long-term outcomes such as heart disease.
